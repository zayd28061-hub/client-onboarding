The guarantee

At the end of 90 days you own a named positioning, a documented body of your own expertise, a content system that runs, and 90 days of published work. If any of those do not exist, the work continues at no extra charge.

"I don't guarantee revenue, reach or follower growth. Anyone who does is guessing with your money."

An existence guarantee, not a results guarantee. His own documents forbid the second, and this buyer is repelled by it.

Payment

Pay in full

$7,000. Cleanest, and the best cash position. Never discount for it — add something instead. A discount teaches the buyer the price is soft and every conversation after becomes a haggle.

Split pay

Deposit plus instalments across the 90 days. Removes affordability without lowering the price. Terms never appear on the page — they come out on the call, answering a real objection.

Say this on every call: "Don't price this monthly, it isn't a retainer. A retainer buys output that stops when you stop paying. This buys things you keep." Divide by three and they land on "cheap," and this buyer reads price as a signal.
Objections

Answer, then stop talking.

From the SOP's affluent track: avoid apologetic or timid statements, set a decisive tone, clarity over detail. A long answer to an objection reads as justification.

The five that decide it

"Who have you done this for?"

"Privately, for years. And publicly — I did three of these on camera recently, unedited, with founders who'd never met me. Go and watch one. What happens in that hour is what happens in the first session."

The strongest position available to anyone selling anything: stop arguing and show them.

"I've bought programmes before."

"What was it, and what happened?" Listen. "Was the information wrong?" Usually no. "So you knew what to do and stopped doing it. That's the part I work on."

The strongest handle in the bank, because the buyer supplies the proof.

"Shouldn't the business be the brand, not me?"

"Who recommended you to your last client? A person. Businesses don't speak, people do.

And on whether this makes the business harder to sell — buyers pay for demand that continues without you. Right now your demand runs through whoever happens to remember you. That's the part that's hard to sell."

The central objection for this buyer. Pre-empt it in the webinar or lose them silently.

"I don't have time. I'm running a business."

"You make time for everything with a deadline. This has none, which is why it's the only thing that slips without anyone noticing. That's not a time problem. And it's why production is handled — I need your presence, not your hours."

"Will this make me look like one of those people?"

"No. If it did I'd have the same problem with it you do. Nothing goes out with your name on it that you haven't approved in writing. Not as an extra — as the default. The test is whether you'd forward it to your biggest client."

Michael's own conduct answers this before any words do.
