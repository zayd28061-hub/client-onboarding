What has to be true

Protect these

  • The email list. Unknown last-send date. Mail 90-day openers only, 100 a day, ramping. One blast to a cold 900 burns the sending domain and there is no budget to recover it.
  • Who gets the three seats. 68% of his audience have nothing to sell. Wrong participants means the right proof for the wrong buyer.
  • No pitch in the lives. The absence of a pitch is what makes them credible.
  • Three seats means three. False scarcity is detected instantly by this buyer.

Highest-yield hours

  • Manual DMs. Registrations only reach the expected column if the warm list is worked by hand. Budget six hours. Easiest thing to skip, biggest cost if skipped.
  • The last five minutes of each live. That is the clip and the testimonial.
  • Clipping within 24 hours. Momentum decays fast.
  • The registration page headline. Outcome first, belief underneath.
